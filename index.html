how do I get it on the actual TV?
  • HDMI / second display: drag the TV window onto the big screen and press F for fullscreen.
  • Chromecast: Chrome menu → Cast → Cast tab, and pick the TV window.
  • Smart TV browser: open this same page on the TV and go to #tv, then keep the remote on your phone — both must be the same browser profile, so this works best with a laptop plugged into the TV.
